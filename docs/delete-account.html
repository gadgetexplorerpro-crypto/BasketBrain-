Google Play Data Safety — Account Deletion

Delete Your BasketBrain Account & All Data

Application: BasketBrain (com.aistudio.basketbrain.kxmprz)

Option 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open the BasketBrain Android app and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to the Profile tab → Data & Account Management.
  3. Tap Delete Account & All Cloud Data and confirm. All Firestore records and your account profile are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Web Account Deletion Request Form

If you no longer have the app installed, submit this form to permanently delete your BasketBrain account and all associated cloud data within 7 business days:

Your deletion request has been prepared. Your email client will open to send the verified request to privacy@basketbrain.app, or you may email us directly from your registered Google address.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and `/users/{userId}` profile document
  • All household member profiles (`members`)
  • All planned meals (`planned_meals`), pantry items (`pantry_items`), grocery lists (`grocery_items`), and purchase records (`purchases`)
  • Retention period after deletion: 0 days (deleted immediately upon processing).
